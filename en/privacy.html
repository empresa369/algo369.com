Privacy

Which personal data passes through this website, who receives it and what you can ask for.

We only write down what actually happens. Before writing it down, we checked the code of the pages.

In short

The essentials in five lines

  • We use no cookies, no local storage, no pixels, no advertising and no traffic analytics tools.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records your IP address when you open a page.
  • The fonts come from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google’s servers, and Google receives your IP address.
  • If you write to us at hello@algo369.com, the email passes through Cloudflare and is stored in a Gmail mailbox.
  • If you buy something, the purchase and payment go through Gumroad, which passes the purchase details on to us.

When you visit the website

What happens on every visit

The website consists of static pages. It has no user accounts, no forms and no comments.

Hosting: GitHub Pages

The pages are served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the USA. As with any request on the internet, your browser sends your IP address, the address of the page you open and the usual browser headers.

GitHub states that, whenever someone visits a GitHub Pages website, it logs and stores the visitor’s IP address for security purposes, whether or not they have a GitHub account. We have no access to these records.

  • Purpose: to serve the pages and protect the service.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the website online securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; under the FADP, an overriding interest, Art. 31 FADP).
  • More information: GitHub’s privacy statement.

Fonts: Google Fonts

The Inter and Playfair Display fonts are loaded directly from Google’s servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), operated by Google LLC in the USA. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address, the requested address and the browser headers, including the browser type and the page you came from.

Google states that the Google Fonts API does not set or store cookies and that Google uses the IP address to respond to the request and for security purposes.

  • Purpose: so that the text looks the way it was designed for the website.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; under the FADP, Art. 31 FADP).
  • More information: Google Fonts FAQ and Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and traffic analytics

We use no cookies of our own, no local storage, no tracking pixels, no traffic analytics tools and no advertising. The pages load no scripts. That is also why we show you no cookie notice: there is nothing for you to consent to.

Links to other websites

Some links lead elsewhere, for example to Gumroad or, in future, to affiliate programmes. If you click one of them, you are on another company’s website, subject to its privacy rules. Your browser usually tells it that you came from algo369.com.

The purpose of an affiliate link is to let the programme know that you came through us. To do this, the programme records the click and may store a cookie in your browser, on its website, not on ours. These programmes send us reports on clicks, purchases and commissions. We do not ask them for any data that identifies you. We explain everything else on the affiliate links page.

When you write to us

Emails to hello@algo369.com

Emails to hello@algo369.com are forwarded via Cloudflare (Email Routing by Cloudflare, Inc., USA) to a Gmail mailbox (Google, USA), where they are stored. Cloudflare states that it neither stores nor accesses the content of forwarded messages; Gmail stores them like any mailbox.

We keep what you send us: your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of the message.

  • Purpose: to reply to you and deal with your request.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, if it concerns a purchase, the performance of the contract (point (b)); under the FADP, Art. 31 FADP.
  • How long: for as long as is needed to deal with the request. After that we delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

We do not use your email address for newsletters or advertising.

When you buy

Purchases through Gumroad

The shop sells through Gumroad, Inc. (USA). The order and payment take place on Gumroad’s website, under Gumroad’s privacy policy. We do not see or store your card details.

Gumroad passes on to us the purchase details we need to process and follow up the sale, for example your email address and the product you bought.

  • Purpose: to deliver the product, answer questions, process refunds and keep the accounts.
  • Legal basis: the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the legal obligations relating to accounting and tax (point (c)); under the FADP, Art. 31 FADP.
  • How long: for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax purposes.
Where the data goes

Recipients and disclosure abroad

We do not sell or rent out personal data. The only recipients are the providers named above: GitHub, Google, Cloudflare and Gumroad. All of them are based in the USA.

  • GitHub, Google and Cloudflare state that they participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The European Commission considers the protection provided by organisations certified under this framework to be adequate (Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795). The Swiss Federal Council does the same for organisations certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Annex 1 to the Data Protection Ordinance, DPO, SR 235.11).
  • Gumroad states that it stores the data in the USA and uses the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission for transfers from the European Economic Area.
Your rights

What you can ask for

You can ask us, free of charge, for:

  • Access: whether we process data about you, and which data (Art. 25 FADP; Art. 15 GDPR).
  • Rectification: correction of inaccurate data (Art. 32 FADP; Art. 16 GDPR).
  • Erasure and restriction: deletion of data or restriction of its use (Art. 32 FADP; Art. 17 and 18 GDPR).
  • Objection: to object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 30 and 32 FADP; Art. 21 GDPR).
  • Data portability: to receive the data you gave us in a common electronic format (Art. 28 FADP; Art. 20 GDPR).

Write to hello@algo369.com. We reply within 30 days. If we need to check that it is really you, we only ask for what is strictly necessary.

If you are not satisfied

In Switzerland, you can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC): edoeb.admin.ch. In the European Union, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work (Art. 77 GDPR); in Portugal, for example, that is the CNPD.

Changes

If something changes

If we change something on the website that affects what happens to the data, for example if we host the fonts ourselves or add a new service, we will update this page beforehand or on the same day.
